Introduction & Overview

Welcome to QIVO: 8 Ball Pool Billiards ("QIVO", "the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle, protect, and process data when you download, install, play, or make purchases within QIVO on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store).

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy in strict compliance with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1), the Google Play Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2

Information We Collect & Why

QIVO collects minimal data required to deliver realistic multiplayer billiards physics, cross-device cloud progress, global rankings, and tournament rewards:

Category Data Elements Purpose & Basis
Authentication & Profile Player ID, Gamer Tag, Avatar URL, Email (when using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In) Account synchronization, player identity, and tournament bracket pairing.
Game Progression Coins, Cash, unlocked cues, win/loss stats, tournament trophies, season pass tier Cloud saving via Firebase Cloud Firestore to prevent progress loss across devices.
Gaming Services Player ID and High Scores submitted to Apple Game Center / Google Play Games Publishing official global and weekly league leaderboards & unlocking achievements.
Technical & Crash Diagnostics Device model, OS version, frame rate metrics, crash reports Ensuring smooth 60/120 FPS physics simulation and game stability.
Playing as Guest / Offline Mode

You can enjoy QIVO without linking any social or email account. In Guest mode, a randomized identifier is created locally on your device with zero personal data transmitted.

3

In-App Purchases & Financial Billing

QIVO offers optional virtual goods including coins, cash packs, custom cues, and the Royal Golden VIP Pass.

  • iOS Transactions: Handled exclusively by Apple StoreKit through your Apple ID account.
  • Android Transactions: Handled exclusively by Google Play Billing.
  • Zero Financial Storage: We do NOT collect, view, process, or store credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.
  • Restore Purchases: In compliance with Apple Guideline 3.1.1, you can restore all previously purchased non-consumables (such as the VIP Pass) anytime via the "RESTORE PURCHASES" button located inside the in-game Royal Store.
4

Third-Party Service Providers

We work with industry-standard, certified infrastructure partners who adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Apple Inc. (iOS Services): Apple Sign-In, Game Center (GameKit), and StoreKit. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (Firebase & Play Services): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (database), Google Play Games. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Optional): Facebook Login for challenging friends. Meta Privacy Policy.
5

Mandatory Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and privacy laws, you possess the unconditional right to delete your account and all associated data permanently.

How to Delete Your Data:

1. Inside the App: Go to Profile > Cloud Save Settings > tap "Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Progress".
2. Via Email Request: Send an email to ferdayounes@gmail.com with your Player ID or registered email. All cloud data, wallet balances, and match history will be permanently deleted from our servers within 14 business days.

6

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA)

QIVO is designed as a general-audience billiards sports simulation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, we will promptly delete such data from our databases.

7

Data Security & Encryption

We implement multi-layered security measures including:

  • All network communications between the Game and servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Access to Cloud Firestore databases is protected by granular, role-based Firebase Security Rules ensuring players can only access their authorized profile records.
  • No sensitive auth tokens or passwords are ever stored in plain text.
